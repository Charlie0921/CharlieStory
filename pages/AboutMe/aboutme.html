My Journey

2013
CURIOSITY & CREATION SOUTH KOREA
When smartphones first emerged, I couldn’t afford one as a student and often felt left behind watching others explore new technology that connected people in ways I couldn’t.
Out of curiosity, I built a simple smartphone mock-up using circuits and LEDs to simulate its glow. That early experiment sparked my lifelong interest in how technology bridges people, leading me to start a tech blog that has since grown to over 650K views.
2017
GLOBAL SERVICE & SUSTAINABILITY CHINA
During a school trip to rural China, I saw villagers on mountain slopes facing severe water shortages, traveling long distances each day to secure clean water.
I joined Habitat for Humanity to help build a water well, moving bricks and learning firsthand how engineering can directly improve lives. It taught me that technology and compassion together can create sustainable change.
2020
IMMIGRANTS & EDUCATION SOUTH KOREA
Many children from immigrant families in South Korea felt difficult to learn English or their parents’ native language due to financial barriers.
I volunteered at the Seongnam Immigrant Center, leading weekly English classes that used simple games and conversations to make learning fun. Watching their progress taught me how education and empathy can empower others.
2022
MENTORSHIP & LEARNING UNITED STATES
Many first-year students at Penn State felt challenging with calculus concepts essential for computer science due to abstract theories and limited peer support.
I became a Learning Assistant for MATH 141, running weekly Q&A sessions and collaborating with instructors to make lessons clearer and more inclusive. It showed me the impact of mentorship and interactive learning.
2023
INNOVATION & INCLUSION UNITED STATES
Campus opportunities like scholarships, competitions, and mentoring programs were scattered across multiple platforms, creating an information gap among students.
I co-developed BeyondClass website, a React and Firebase web app that centralized these resources. Our project became a Top 6 Finalist in the 2023 Inc.U Competition, proving that thoughtful design can make education more equitable.
2024
LEADERSHIP & ENGINEERING SOUTH KOREA
While working as an Ammunition Company Leader in the Republic of Korea Air Force, one of the soldiers suggested a problem that many people on base had difficulty coordinating laundry schedules, often waiting unnecessarily for machines to become available.
Taking that idea forward, I designed and co-developed Tanyak Laundry, a React and Supabase web app that displayed real-time machine availability and sent notifications. Once deployed to over 60 residents, the app increased laundry efficiency by 90%, proving how collaborative problem-solving and technology can improve daily life even in military settings.
